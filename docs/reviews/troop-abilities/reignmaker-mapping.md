# ReignMaker ability mapping

Generated by `node scripts/audit-reignmaker-abilities.mjs`. This review selects useful abstractions; it makes no claim of runtime support or complete Pathfinder simulation.

The [assignable catalogue](catalogue.md) defines the proposed game effects and limits. Source slugs identify evidence in this table; they are not runtime matching rules.

Reviewed 2026-09-23, source commit `b8bb51f44b6b317021c05ed8c76e3788ffee435a`, with per-file hashes. The snapshot contains **97 registry abilities and 6 doctrine grants**. All have a disposition.

The training ladder has **42 entries**, including **39 tactical choices** and three campaign logistics entries. Grant bundles and prerequisites resolve to reviewed entries.

Dispositions: abstracted: 62; baseline: 20; omit: 6; defer: 7; reaction: 8.

- **abstracted:** Assign shared abilities and accept the stated simplifications.
- **baseline:** Keep the existing attack, movement, role, or stat abstraction; add no ability.
- **omit:** Intentionally leave this detail in the source description.
- **defer:** A distinctive mechanic needs a later decision; grant nothing automatically.
- **reaction:** Record a separate future reaction assignment; grant nothing until reactions exist.

## Common generated rules

ReignMaker generates Troop Defenses from HP thresholds outside the 97-entry registry. Classify it as baseline: use Battlefield’s single hex and four-Health abstraction. Form Up and Troop Movement already appear in the registry. Omit segment loss, individual-target immunity, and formation resizing; preserve source HP and traits as evidence.

## Import findings

- ReignMaker is supplementary evidence. The broader saved review contains 200 source actors and 1,384 actions; Siphoning Grip and regeneration inform the catalogue even though the registry has no corresponding definition.
- Training copies system.slug to action items, but the creature ability builder copies name and mechanics without preserving that slug. Read mechanics and portable explicit assignments; actor names, item names, slugs, and UUIDs are recognition hints only.
- Doctrine grants include five effect items and one reaction action. Inspect both item types. Actor slugs use doctrine-ability- followed by the grant ID; the snapshot saves that value and the underlying definition.
- The actual doctrine code requires dominance for all six grants. The older doctrine reference document differs from current definitions; use the saved code and item text as evidence.
- Keep source action, passive, and reaction economy in the audit. A proposed abstraction may deliberately change a passive into a paid Guard effect, but must label that change. Signal the Advance requires recipient reactions despite being a paid source action.
- Prepared Foundry statistics may already include rule-element bonuses. Deduplicate imported bonuses against prepared stats before applying Exploit, Resolve, or auras; never add a modifier just because its ability text survived.
- Bundle children and parent tactics collapse to one assignment. Examples include Combat Medics/Battlefield Medicine, Shield Discipline/Raise Shields, and the three siege crew training abilities.
- Abstracted means a proposed shared game effect, not implemented support. Deferred entries retain an explicit review note; omitted and baseline entries add no new ability.
- Keep historical source text, exact hashes, and deliberate omissions. Persist selected catalogue IDs, trigger, local attack attachment, prerequisites, and flavor label separately from source identity.
