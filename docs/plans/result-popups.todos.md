# Result popups

Date: 2026-09-18.

- The board floats the word a roll came to over the piece it lands on: Hit, Miss and their criticals over an attack's target; Success, Failure and their criticals over the piece that rolled any other check; Repulsed over an attacker that fails its repulse save; Routed over a piece that routs.
- A blow reads as its result and then what it cost: Hit, then "−2" with a heart, then "−1" with a flag if the brace failed. The brace against a wound shows no word of its own (`reads: 'brace'`); the flag is its result. Every wound and disorder change shows this way, whatever caused it, so a Rally shows "+1" with a flag in green and a round's persistent wounds show their hearts.
- Icons with numbers were chosen over words: they need no translation, they are short, and they name the two bars on the token. Signs follow the bars, which count what remains, so a wound is "−1". The heart and flag are drawn as vectors in `PopupLayer.ts`; the repo has no art for them.
- The record lists every roll before any change of state. The presentation moves each effect up to follow the last word over its own piece.
- The words are live text in Carter One (SIL Open Font License, bundled at `public/fonts/carter-one/`). The Photoshop sprites used Astounder Squared, whose Adobe Fonts licence forbids bundling the font file. Live text also leaves room for localization and for new words.
- The engine records where a check lands (`LogEntry.lands`) on the lines whose roller is not the piece affected: strikes, free strikes, shots, Blast, Healing, and the repulse save. Every other check lands on its roller. `checkResolved` carries the landing.
- A repulse save the attacker passes shows no word. The Miss over the target already told the story, and a green Success over the attacker after a red Miss reads as a contradiction.
- A swing at a wall shows Success or Failure over the attacker, since no piece receives it.
- Colour follows the degree, as in the PSD: green for a success, red for a failure, orange for Repulsed and Routed. A loss on a bar is red and a gain is green.
- Popups take turns in event order, 1 s apart, and each lasts 2 s: Miss over the target, then Repulsed over the attacker. With four or more waiting the gap closes to 500 ms. Every popup waits until the pieces stop moving, so a charge reads its result where it ends.
- The wounds and the disorder of one blow share a popup, side by side. It follows its word at 85% of the size and swells over 400 ms against the word's 250 ms. The rise is eased out and covers 0.75 of a hex, so the word has cleared its own height when the bars arrive beneath it.
- Every word lives in `src/app/result-words.ts`, English only. Marked `proto:`.
- A cast the target saves against never shows the save's degree. A critical success shows Resisted over the target, red and 25% larger, since a cast that came to nothing is the caster's failure. A plain success still leaves the target Frightened, so it shows Frightened; a failure shows the flags it cost, then Stunned and Held for the stronger casts.
- Conditions are events now: `conditionGained` for frightened, stunned, rooted (shown as Held), pinned and exposed, derived in `ActionResolutionService` from the state across the commit. Orange, full size, and the conditions one act leaves share a popup. So a critical miss shows Exposed over the attacker, and a pinning shot shows Pinned. The root a unit takes on itself with Take cover raises no event.
- Audit of every act against the popups, 2026-09-18. Added: Suppressed (Suppress and Pin set it hit or miss, and it showed nothing), Marked for persistent damage from a wrathful hit, and Blocked in red over a warded piece when its aegis wastes an attack; an attack that passes the aegis shows only its own result. A Pin therefore reads Hit or Miss, the bars, then Suppressed and Pinned together. A condition counts as gained when the value holding it changes, so a second shooter's pin on a pinned piece shows too.
- Left as they are: Healing, Rally, break off and keep hold show Success or Failure, since a failure there leaves nothing else to show. A charge shows Exposed over the charger, a price it chose; open for play whether that is noise.
- Open for the rules, not decided here: Pin lands automatically, hit or miss, like Suppress. Suppress by volume fits the fiction. Pin is the stronger effect, a hold at Volley + 10 with no roll and no save, and gating it on the shot hitting would make rung 3 a gamble where it is now a purchase.
- Open for play: whether helpful states (Inspired, Warded, Sure strike) deserve green words of their own, and whether criticals and Routed should be loud like Resisted.
- Vitest (529 tests), `npm run check`, `npx vite build` and `npm run build:foundry` pass. The Foundry build now copies `public/fonts`. No screenshot was taken.
- The board loads its icons from `public/art/condition-icons/`: two bars and eight conditions. A bar's icon follows its number and a condition's icon leads its word; Routed carries one too. All twenty icons in `art-src/condition-icons/ICONS.md` were generated on 2026-09-18 and ship as 512 px WebP at quality 80, with the 1024 px generations kept in `art-src/condition-icons/originals/`. The pink placeholders and the Pathfinder reference images were removed. Ten buff icons and `dead.webp` are in the folder and unused until those popups exist.
- A piece carries a column of status icons up its right side, from the corner Guard's shield used to hold to just under the flag (`STATUS_COLUMN` in `Token.ts`). `statusesOn` in `Battle.svelte` lists them from the unit's state in a fixed order: Guard, then what enemies did (pinned, rooted, suppressed, stunned, frightened, exposed, persistent), then its own side's casts (aegis, warded, stoneskin, sure strike, wrath, hasted, sure footing, burst of speed, inspired). This puts all twenty generated icons to use except `dead.webp`. Guard now shows `guard.webp`, the three locked shields, where it showed the Block action's shield.
- One or two icons keep the old corner size, 30% of the hex. A third shrinks them toward 20%, and beyond that they overlap, newest on top.
- A status joining the column announces itself (`STATUS_INTRO`): it fades in over the piece at 85% of the hex while growing from 60% to full, holds for half a second, then shrinks over 400 ms into its slot. Several joining in one commit take turns 800 ms apart. A piece that mounts with statuses plays nothing, so a load or a join does not set the whole field off. The icon aimed at a piece (attack, charge, the red cross) still snaps, and draws over the column's bottom slot while it shows.
- Open for play: whether the column reads better on the left, clear of the aim icon; and whether a lapsing status deserves an exit.
- 2026-09-19: a popup lasts 3.5 s, up from 2 s, because the words were hard to read in time. The entrance is unchanged: the same pop, fade-in, and 0.75-hex rise over the first 2 s, after which the word holds still. It fades over the last 500 ms and shrinks to 60% as it goes. The 1 s stagger stays, so words now overlap in time; a word arriving over a piece lifts the earlier words over that piece by its own height, which keeps a Hit clear of its bars. Open for play: whether a Blast's long run now holds the board too long.
