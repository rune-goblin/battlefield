Complete pencil terrain library — 96 sprites

16 each: hills, swamps, forests, plains, desert, mountains.
Each of the first five types preserves its earlier 12 variants and adds four new variants, numbered 13–16. All 16 mountain variants are new. The package therefore includes 60 earlier sprites and 36 new sprites.

Each atlas: 1536 x 1536 pixels, 4 columns x 4 rows.
Each cell: 384 x 384 pixels.
Origin: (0,0). Slice every 384 pixels with zero inter-cell spacing.
Order: left to right, then top to bottom.
Padding: at least 24 pixels of pure white inside every cell.
All 96 individual PNGs exactly match their sheet slices.

Opaque pure-white background with neutral gray pencil linework. Use Multiply blending. PNG and WebP versions are lossless. The sheets folder includes a combined manifest and separate manifests. The trees key means forests; swamp means swamps.
