Complete pencil terrain library — generation notes

Final library: 96 sprites, sixteen each for hills, swamps, forests, plains, desert and mountains.
This revision adds 36 new drawings: four per biome and sixteen new mountain formations. The prior 60 biome sprites remain unchanged.
All six sheets use 4 x 4 cells at 384 x 384 pixels; each sheet is 1536 x 1536 pixels. Every cell has at least 24 pixels of pure-white padding. All individual PNGs match their atlas slices exactly.
Created with built-in imagegen. The final new sources were normalized to neutral grayscale and pure white, then packed into uniform cells with proportions intact. Mountain cut boundaries account for the source drawings' unequal widths.

Terrain occupies a real two-dimensional map footprint: peaks can stand behind and in front of one another rather than all forming a horizontal skyline. Thin neutral medium-gray contours, a few confident irregular creases along ridges, large mostly unmarked rock faces, occasional sparse short parallel hatch strokes. The level of detail and shading should match the approved biome sheet. Avoid dense engraving, micro-hatching, heavy outlines, dark filled shadows, photorealism or 3D shading.
Soft broken foothill contours taper into white. No enclosed ground disks or hard baselines.
