# Prompt specification

The built-in image workflow generated sixteen registered key poses per effect using the supplied fantasy spell icons as the style reference. Each prompt required one persistent magical object, fixed center registration, continuous particle paths, gradual scale and intensity changes, consistent perspective and lighting, a 4×4 row-major key-pose plan from 10% birth through peak to near-transparent residue, and no background, checkerboard, labels, borders, text, UI, or watermark.

Effects and palettes:

- Blast: compact fireball, molten orange, white-hot gold, ember red, charcoal stone.
- Heal: green bubbles and mint wisps rising from a golden pool; no cross or plus.
- Control: sapphire runic control field with restrained bronze-gold.
- Buff attacks: orange-gold weapon glint and controlled energy arcs.
- Buff defenses: sapphire shield-shaped hex ward with bronze-gold detail.
- Buff movement: cyan wing-like wind ribbon and left-to-right trails; no healing symbols.
