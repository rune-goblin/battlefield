# Generation prompt set

All six sheets used the supplied spell-icon sheet as their fantasy-rendering reference and the approved blast sheet as their temporal-layout reference.

- Blast: one fireball progressing through ember birth, growth, forward surge, impact compression, peak explosion, fragment expansion, sparks, and fade.
- Heal: one golden ground pool progressing through activation, rising emerald bubbles, mint spiral, peak healing glint, separation, and fade.
- Control: one sapphire arcane field progressing through mote birth, rune tracing, intersecting ring lock, peak pulse, fragmentation, unwind, and fade.
- Buff attacks: one orange-gold energy edge progressing through glint birth, curved slash sweep, intersecting peak arcs, sparks, and fade.
- Buff defenses: one sapphire hex ward progressing through line tracing, shield assembly, energy fill, impact ripple, fragment separation, and fade.
- Buff movement: one cyan wind wisp progressing through birth, curl, acceleration, wing-like peak, trailing ribbons, and fade.

Shared constraints: strict 4×4 row-major sequence; fixed camera and center anchor; actual transparent background; one continuous effect rather than variations; no text, labels, borders, watermark, checkerboard, black backdrop, UI frame, or scene.
